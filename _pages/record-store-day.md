# Record Store Day in My Vinyl+

Record Store Day is the one morning a year when a list matters more than a shelf. My Vinyl+ carries the release lists inside the app you already use to track what you own, so the plan and the collection are never in two different places.

## What it does

- **Browse the release list.** Every title on the Record Store Day and RSD Black Friday lists, with artist, title, label and format, searchable by any of them.
- **Build a wishlist.** Mark the titles you want, in list order, and keep it separate from the collection you already own.
- **Check what you already have.** A title you own is visible as owned before you leave for the shop, which is the difference between buying a record and buying a duplicate.
- **Find the shops taking part.** Participating stores appear on a map, so the nearest participating shop is a short list rather than a search.
- **Share the list.** Send your wishlist to whoever is shopping for you, so the gift is the record you wanted and not the one you already own.

## The 2026 Black Friday list

Record Store Day published its Black Friday list on 2 November 2026 for the 27 November event. The US list carries 188 titles: 121 exclusives, 47 RSD First releases and 20 limited-run or regional titles. They are in My Vinyl+ now, under the Record Store Day section, ready to wishlist before the morning.

The Black Friday list Record Store Day publishes is the US list; the titles are sold at participating independent shops in the United States, and shops in other countries decide for themselves what to bring in. The main Record Store Day list, which runs in April, is published per country, and those country lists are in the app too.

## How to find it

Open My Vinyl+ and go to the Record Store Day section from Discover. The release list, the wishlist and the store map live there. Nothing needs setting up, and the release data is free.
