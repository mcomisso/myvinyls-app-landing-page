### Version 1.4.0

- Just need Platinum for now? Watch 2 ads to use every Platinum feature for 10 minutes, once every 24 hours.
- Or buy a 24-hour pass: one payment, no subscription.
- Purchases are easier to restore after reinstalling or moving to a new phone.
- Search is ready to type as soon as the filters open.

---

### Version 1.3.3

- Artist, label and album names now appear without Discogs' extra numbers, such as "Label (2)".

---

### Version 1.3.2

- Crash fixes and stability improvements.
- Premium plans load more reliably.

---

### Version 1.3.1

- Now in 33 languages, with a Language setting on Android 13 and later.
- Long-press records in your collection or wantlist to select several, then move or remove them together.
- Swipe a wantlist record to remove it, with Undo.
- Tap the current tab again to jump back to the top.
- Fixed missing translations.

---

### Version 1.3.0

- A fresh look with a new theme, typeface and Discover carousels.
- Seven new widgets, including Collection Value and Album Mosaic.
- Insights is open to everyone, with value history and drill-downs.
- Export to Excel, PDF or CSV, wantlist included.
- Listen to track previews, with controls on your lock screen.
- Get an alert when your collection reaches new heights.
- Privacy mode hides values on Discover and in widgets.

---

### Version 1.2.1

- Fixed a crash when scrolling through some artists' discographies, such as artists credited more than once on the same record.

---

### Version 1.2.0

- Choose the photos on your 3D record: touch & hold an image to use it as the cover, the label or disc, or the back.
- Grade your copy's media and sleeve right from the record page.
- See what a record is worth in every condition, from Mint to Poor.
- Images now come before the tracklist.
- Buy now shows only for records on your wantlist.

---

### Version 1.1.1

- Artist pages now show the artist's logo over their photo.
- Artist photos now open full screen instead of behind the biography.
- Fixed the back of 3D CD cases flickering.
- A simpler About page.

---

### Version 1.1.0

- See your records in 3D, lit like a listening room.
- Artist logos on your records and in Insights, formerly Marketplace.
- Layouts made for tablets and foldables, with smoother transitions.
- Faster, more secure Discogs sign-in, and your collection syncs as soon as you sign in.
- Correct badges for vinyl singles and EPs.
- Faster startup and many fixes.
