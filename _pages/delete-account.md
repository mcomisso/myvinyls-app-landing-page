# Delete My Vinyl+ account data

You can request deletion of your My Vinyl+ account and associated personal data, or ask us to remove selected data while keeping the rest. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

[Email a deletion request to website@myvinyls.app](mailto:website@myvinyls.app?subject=My%20Vinyl%2B%20data%20deletion%20request)

Use the subject **My Vinyl+ data deletion request**. Tell us whether you use iPhone, Android, or both, and whether you want all My Vinyl+ data deleted or only specific information. If you use Discogs with My Vinyl+, you can include your Discogs username to help us understand your request.

Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens or full payment details. We may ask for limited additional information to verify that the records belong to you. A Discogs username alone does not identify every My Vinyl+ installation or purchase.

## What happens next

We will confirm your request, verify the relevant account or app records, and explain its scope and expected completion time. We will delete the My Vinyl+ personal records we can associate with you and request deletion from our service providers where applicable.

Depending on the features you used, this can include account or profile information, device and notification registrations, purchase-service customer records, identifiable analytics or diagnostics, and information you sent to support. You can also ask to remove a specific contribution or message.

We will confirm what was deleted. If a record cannot be linked to you, or particular information must be retained, we will explain what remains, why, and its applicable retention period. We do not promise that every system has the same retention period or that anonymous aggregate information can be traced back to one person.

## Discogs and store subscriptions

Deleting My Vinyl+ data does not delete your Discogs account, Discogs collection or wantlist. Discogs manages those records separately. To delete a Discogs account, use [Discogs account settings](https://www.discogs.com/settings).

Deleting My Vinyl+ data does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, or remove records held independently by those stores. You can manage billing through [Apple subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/118428) or [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). We can handle your data request even if you have already removed the app.

## Data on your devices

Removing an account from My Vinyl+ signs it out locally; it is not a request to delete server records. Remove the app or clear its local data on each device if you also want to remove local copies. Device or cloud backups are managed separately through Apple or Google.

Using the app again can create new app records or sync data again from Discogs. Let us know if you want help identifying the local steps that apply to your devices.

Read the [My Vinyl+ Privacy Policy](/privacypolicy/) for more information.
